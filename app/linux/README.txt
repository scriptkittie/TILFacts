TILFacts for Linux
==================

Today I learned, condensed: fresh facts from Reddit's r/todayilearned.

To add it to your apps menu (do this once):
  Open a terminal in this folder and type:  ./install.sh
  Then open your apps menu and click TILFacts.

To just run it without installing:
  Open a terminal in this folder and type:  ./TILFacts
  (Some file managers also let you right-click TILFacts and choose "Run as a Program".)

Your web browser opens with the facts. Close the tab when you're done and
TILFacts closes itself a few seconds later.

To remove it from your apps menu:  ./install.sh --remove

Needs an internet connection. Works on 64-bit Linux with a desktop
(Ubuntu, Fedora, Mint, Debian and so on).
