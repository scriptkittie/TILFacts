# TILFacts

Condenses TIL into soylent TIL: the newest posts from Reddit's
[r/todayilearned](https://www.reddit.com/r/todayilearned/), with the "TIL that..."
taken out, so all that's left is the fact.

TILFacts is a little old project, first written on June 30, 2015 as a Python
command-line script that printed the day's TIL posts in a terminal and asked
"Would you like to close?" It has since had some love: it's now a small app that
opens the facts in your browser, with a nutrition-facts label for each batch,
themes and a saved list. It still asks the same question at the end.

**[Download TILFacts](https://github.com/scriptkittie/TILFacts/releases/latest)**

## How to use it

### Windows

1. On the download page, under **Assets**, click **TILFacts.exe**.
2. Double-click it. Your web browser opens with today's facts.

The first time, Windows may say **"Windows protected your PC."** Click
**More info**, then **Run anyway**. You only do this once.

### Linux

1. On the download page, click **TILFacts-linux-x64.tar.gz** (most PCs) or
   **TILFacts-linux-arm64.tar.gz** (Raspberry Pi and other ARM computers).
2. Extract it, open a terminal in the `TILFacts` folder, and type `./install.sh`.
3. Open your apps menu and click **TILFacts**.

### When you're done

Close the browser tab and TILFacts closes itself. Or scroll to the bottom, where it
asks **"Would you like to close?"**, and press **Yes**.

## What you can do

- **Save** facts to keep them, and **Copy all** from your **Saved** list.
- **Copy** a fact with its source link to send to someone.
- Click the **source** to read where a fact came from, or **Thread** to see the
  Reddit discussion.
- **Search** the facts on the page.
- Change how many facts you get and where they come from (newest, most popular
  today, best of all time) on the label.
- Pick a look: Auto, Label, Night, Carton, Oat or Terminal.

Facts are what people posted on Reddit, and TILFacts doesn't check them, so follow
the source before you repeat one at dinner.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Reddit asked us to slow down"**: wait a minute and press **Try again**.
- **"Couldn't reach Reddit"**: check your internet connection. Some work and school
  networks block Reddit.
- **"TILFacts has closed"**: double-click TILFacts again.
